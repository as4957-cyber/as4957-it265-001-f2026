# IT265 Module 2: Concept Pitch Cards

Copy this card for each of your two or three shortlisted concepts. Aim to explain each in about one minute. Use your [idea bank](./01-idea-bank.html) as a starting point.

## Pitch Card

**Working title:**Retro Kick Games

**Player role and situation:** Player controls soccer player in a retro style game

**Repeated decision or action:** The player moves, passes, shoots, and defends against other team

**Goal, pressure, or ending:** Goal is to score more goals than the other team before match ends

**Hook:** The game uses simple retro pixel art style with fast gameplay and special effects to make matches more fun.

**Smallest useful physical prototype:**Small soccer stadium containing two players, a ball, basic movement, passing, and shooting

**Question I want listeners to answer:** Does this retro style soccer concept sound interesting and fun enough to play?

Keep the cards together in this document and link its rendered page from your workshop index. They support the journal and treatment rather than adding another graded submission.
